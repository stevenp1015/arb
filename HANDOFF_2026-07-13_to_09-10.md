## 2. THE THREE COLLAPSES

These are the most valuable thing in this period. Each one cost weeks.

### Collapse 1 — the liquidation fade (July 15)

**The claim:** backtest showed **86% win rate, +23bps across 2,478 liquidation events.** Enormous.

**The audit:** sitters excluded from the first calculation — trades where the target never got hit and the position
just sat there. Recomputed with them counted: **actual -0.06% net against a claimed +0.25%.**

**The verdict:** confirmed dead at **-0.099%**, then independently re-measured at **-0.10% per signal.**

**Why it matters:** the 86% was never wrong about the trades it counted. It was wrong about which trades to count.
A signal that never reaches its target is not a neutral non-event — it is a position burning time and paying fees.

### Collapse 2 — pack_rider probation (July 27)

82 out-of-sample events over a clean 14-day run. Mean **-6.7bps per event**, median -2.2, only **39 of 82 positive.**
Down-fires lose **6x more** than up-fires (-11.0 vs -1.7bps). The killer was timeout-down sitters, 30.7% of 12,190 fills.

There is a tempting after-the-fact observation: July 20 onward runs **+3.6bps/event** (21/36 positive) against -15.8
before. It was explicitly flagged as untested and explicitly marked **not to be treated as a pass.** It could be
regime, it could be noise.

**No real-money unlock.** `pack_rider` never traded real money.

### Collapse 3 — the execution-cost wall (July 29 onward)

This is the big one, and it took the most work.

`fp_engine/` found a real signal. A model pooled across 84 coins on June data, testing first passage — after a signal
fires, does price travel far enough in your favour before it travels against you? There is no clock and no holding
period. You just ask which happens first. Result: **wins about 3-5 percentage points more often than chance, and it
holds up on data it was never tuned on.** The launch backtest showed a **54.6% edge against realistic fills.**

Then it was charged for actually trading it:

| Method | Result |
|---|---|
| Quoting passively (maker) | **-0.005% per fill** |
| Paying the spread (taker) | **-0.011% per trade** |
| BTC, closest to breaking even | -0.002% |

And two follow-ups narrowed it further. The cross-venue version of the edge exists **only at near-zero reaction delay**
— +4.05 percentage points at zero delay, **negative by 100 milliseconds.** Whether it survives at the real ~15ms
reaction time is unresolved. And separately: **cancelling your quote fast does not rescue it.** Tested August 1,
rejected. The benefit is ten times smaller than the fee it would cost, and the reason is structural — you get filled
passively *because someone chose to trade against you at that price*, so the bad news is inside the fill itself, not
in an earlier signal you could have raced.

**This is where the period ended as a research question.** The signal is real and too small to pay for.

### The mechanism that made it a verdict on a whole family

The August 11 independent replication of the liq-fade failure (run blind, before reading the Codex verdict, same
answer) found the mechanism, and it generalizes past that one bot:

- Target +0.69% fires 36.7% of the time and realizes **+0.747%** — it overshoots by only 0.057pp.
- Stop -1.18% fires 21.9% of the time and realizes **-1.362%** — it overshoots by **0.182pp**.
- **All 141 stop-outs came in worse than the stop.**

You eat gap-through on every loss and collect almost none of it on wins. **Any fixed-bracket spike-fade on these books
inherits this.** That is a verdict on the entire family, not on one parameter set.

Two more findings from that run that corrected the original hypothesis:

- **"The trash hypothesis is backwards."** The theory was majors absorb and trash dents, so trash should be the good
  bucket. Measured: majors **-0.026%**, trash **-0.133%**, stock perps **-0.177%**. **Deeper books lose less.**
- The loss is not a correlated-sample artifact. Collapsing cascades into 60-second episodes gives 537 independent
  episodes, per-episode mean -0.100%, t = -2.76. It survives the honest-n correction.

### The one bucket that was left money on the table

**41.4% of signals (266) timed out at the hour without ever reaching target.** Of those, **120 were positive at the
hour, averaging +0.257%.** That is the largest single pool of forgone money in the ledger.

It cannot be tested from that ledger — it needs price paths that were never archived. The cheap route is to change
the exit and accumulate forward at ~70 signals/day. **And this is exactly what MoonTrader's "Auto Price Down" does** —
walk the target down on a timer toward a floor. That is the concrete bridge between the failed July work and the
mission statement. Note `liq_paper.py` was stopped Aug 6 and would need restarting.

## 3. WHAT SURVIVED

Not everything died. These held up:

**The SMA ladder rule.** 530 coins, 61,000 setups, **62-64% compliance toward the 200 SMA, 80-82% toward the 250**,
against a 7% baseline. Roughly ten times chance. This was re-confirmed repeatedly and is the most robust
directional finding in the entire period.

**The rung hierarchy.** Slow rungs beat fast ones about two to one. 1D SMA1000 28.2%, 1h SMA500 28.0%, 1D SMA20
21.7%, 1h SMA200 19.2%, every 1m and 5m rung 13-15%. Big daily SMAs really are stronger.

**The alerts are attention tools, not direction tools.** 62 signals graded both ways, with the arrow and against it.
Instant spikes: 52% with, 45% against. Pump ignitions: 43% with, 50% against. Neither direction is money. What they
actually do well is say *where* something is happening within three seconds, across 530 pairs, with venue labels.

**The volume-floor-break tell.** The AKE autopsy found a **volume floor break from $0.1k to $179k preceded the AKE
ignition by five minutes.** That is the single most actionable microstructure finding in the period.

**Two pump species.** Across five monster autopsies (BAY, AKE, BANK, B, GUA) plus a six-token cohort study: (1)
dead-floor resurrections, (2) staircase persistence. Plus a third signal — **decoupling from BTC as a pump tell**,
which held for GMT (rebounded from the 18th percentile) but never for AKE (never coupled).

**Binance perp burst cycles.** Cross-pair bursts at **23:35 and 23:45 UTC**, repeating on a **67-minute interval**
(8:45pm EDT). Scanner deployed.

**The June crime-scene data is still the biggest unexploited asset.** 518GB, tri-venue, dual-timestamped to the
millisecond, one clock. Three studies specced in July, **none ever run:** recover the market makers' literal pricing
function by regression, build a "today is X% June-like" regime dial, and calibrate the Leading Ponzi Detector's priors.
Verdict on record: **KEEP IT.** It cannot be re-recorded.

is the honest version of this failure.

The response was law 7, and it is the most important standing law:

> **A quiet machine must be provably quiet-because-nothing-happened.** Heartbeats, disk guard.
